# ⛽ Fuel Surcharge (FSC) Sensitivity & Geopolitical Risk Modeling (Iran Conflict)

**Executive Financial Impact Analytics Across Parcel, LTL, and Ocean Freight Networks**

An executive financial modeling framework built for and Medical Technology leader to quantify, track, and mitigate the cost impact of energy price volatility and carrier Fuel Surcharge (FSC) index adjustments stemming from Middle East geopolitical disruptions.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary & Impact Analysis

Following Middle East geopolitical conflicts, global crude and US refined fuel prices experienced significant inflation (US Diesel prices spiked **+36%**). This project modeled the weekly P&L variance across multi-modal freight spend (US Domestic Parcel, Domestic LTL/FTL, Ocean Container Shipping), providing executive decision support and setting operational rules of thumb for weekly budget variances.

### 💰 Total Exposure (Feb – Apr Window)
* **Total P&L Impact Identified:** **$221K – $271K+** in incremental Fuel Surcharges across business units.
* **Monthly Run-Rate Trends:** Costs scaled rapidly from **$106K/month in March** to **$165K/month in April**.

## 🔍 Macro Commodity & Index Analysis

1. **Carrier FSC Slope Indexing:** Modeled Parcel Carrier’s global fuel surcharge table updates, analyzing inflection point slope changes to anticipate lag vs. spot price drops (e.g., Brent Crude shifts).
2. **Refined Fuel Market Tracking:** Tracked US Jet Fuel inventory build-ups alongside a **36% surge in US Diesel prices**, directly projecting contract pass-through costs in LTL agreements.
3. **Ocean Freight vs. Market Spot:** Benchmarked the companywide container rates against the Drewry Eastbound Transpacific Index ($6.1K average container cost vs. $7.2K budget limit).
